Referral partner guide

Follow these steps to refer photographers and earn rewards.

i

Your referral journey is simple. Share your code, help the photographer get started, and receive eligible rewards after their payment is verified.

01
STEP 1 · SHARE YOUR CODE

Invite a photographer

Share your unique referral code with photographers who may benefit from Professional Studio. They must use your valid code when signing up.

Photographer benefit: One month of free access after signing up with a valid referral code.
02
STEP 2 · THEY CHOOSE A PLAN

Help them get started

The referred photographer can explore the platform during the free-access period. After that period ends, they must select and pay for a plan to continue using the account.

03
STEP 3 · PAYMENT IS VERIFIED

Earn your subscription commission

You receive 60% of the photographer's first eligible paid subscription. The commission is counted only after the payment has been completed and verified.

04
STEP 4 · SUPPORT THE PHOTOGRAPHER

Earn additional support rewards

You can also provide eligible account-setup assistance by call or on-location support. Each approved support session earns an additional fixed reward.

05
STEP 5 · TRACK YOUR REWARDS

Monitor your referral activity

Use the dashboard to check referral statuses, earned commissions, pending payments, and support-session rewards.

Please read carefully

Commission rules & conditions

These conditions explain when a referral reward is eligible and how it is calculated.

  1. One-time subscription commission Commission applies to the first eligible paid subscription only. Future renewals do not generate recurring referral earnings.
  2. Upgrade eligibility An upward plan upgrade may qualify when it follows the defined plan order and meets the referral terms.
  3. No downgrade commission Moving to a lower-priced plan does not create an additional referral commission.
  4. One month of free access A valid referral code gives the new photographer one month of free access. After the free period, the account is frozen until they select and pay for a plan.
  5. Commission verification Commission is recorded only after the first eligible subscription payment is completed and verified.
  6. Support-session rewards Additional support rewards are paid only for eligible setup-support sessions that have been completed and approved.

The amounts shown in this demo are illustrative. Actual commissions and support-session rewards are calculated according to the approved referral terms and verified account activity.
