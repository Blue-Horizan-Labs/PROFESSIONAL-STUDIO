Getting Started

Follow this simple guide to create your Professional Studio account, set up your profile, and build your photography portfolio.

Step 01

Create Your Account

Start by creating your Professional Studio account. Enter your basic information and create your login credentials.

  • Open the Professional Studio sign-up page.
  • Enter your name and email address.
  • Create a secure password.
  • Complete the registration process.
Step 02

Set Up Your Profile

After creating your account, complete your photographer profile. This information helps clients understand who you are and what you do.

  • Add your profile picture.
  • Add your studio or business name.
  • Enter your contact information.
  • Add your location and experience.
Step 03

Build Your Portfolio

Your portfolio is where potential clients can see your photography style and previous work. Add your best photographs and organize them clearly.

  • Upload your best photography work.
  • Create different work or event categories.
  • Add photographs to each category.
  • Choose your strongest images for your main portfolio.
Step 04

Add Your Services

Add the photography services you offer so clients can understand what they can book through your studio.

  • Add your photography services.
  • Describe each service.
  • Set the available duration.
  • Add relevant packages if required.
Step 05

Add Your Equipment

Showcase the equipment you use for your photography work. This gives clients more information about your capabilities.

  • Add cameras and lenses.
  • Add drones or other equipment.
  • Include important equipment details.
Step 06

Set Your Pricing

Create packages and pricing that match your services. You can provide different options depending on your clients' requirements.

  • Add your service prices.
  • Create different packages.
  • Set package details and inclusions.
  • Add discounts when applicable.
Step 07

Your Studio Is Ready

Once your profile, portfolio, services, equipment, and pricing are complete, your Professional Studio setup is ready.

  • Review your profile.
  • Check your portfolio.
  • Verify your services and pricing.
  • You're ready to start managing your studio.
