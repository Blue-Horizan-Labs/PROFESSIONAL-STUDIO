Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before launching your photography website.

Do I need coding knowledge?

No. Professional Studio is designed for photographers. Set up your portfolio, add your details and upload your work without writing code.

Can I receive online payments?

Yes. Clients can pay booking fees and complete invoices online through supported payment gateways.

Are client galleries password protected?

Yes. Every gallery can have its own password and download permissions.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade your subscription whenever your business needs change.

Is the AI assistant available now?

AI assistant support is an upcoming feature, as shown in the plan details.

How much storage is included?

Starter includes 500 MB, Professional includes 2 GB, and Enterprise includes 10 GB of storage for recent work.
