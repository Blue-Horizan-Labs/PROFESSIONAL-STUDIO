REFERRAL PARTNER

Earn by referring photographers.

You don't have to join the Professional Studio team as an employee to work with us. If you can introduce photographers to Professional Studio, you can apply to become a referral partner.

How it works

01
Contact us

Tell us that you want to become a Professional Studio referral partner.

02
Get your referral code

After approval, we provide you with your unique referral code.

03
Refer photographers

Share your code with photographers who want to use Professional Studio.

04
Earn from successful referrals

When a referred photographer makes their first eligible paid subscription, you receive your referral commission.

YOUR COMMISSION 60%

You receive 60% of the photographer's first eligible paid subscription.

+
Eligible upgrades

An upward plan upgrade can also qualify when it follows the defined plan order.

×
No downgrade commission

Moving to a lower plan does not create additional referral earnings.

1×

Future renewals do not generate another referral commission.

FOR REFERRED PHOTOGRAPHERS

Your referral gives photographers
one month of free access.

When a new photographer creates an account using your referral code, they receive one month of free Professional Studio access. After the free period, their account is frozen until they choose and pay for a plan.
